### 🇺🇸 Abstract (English)
The exponential advancement of Artificial Intelligence (AI) has fueled the proliferation of deepfakes—ultra-realistic synthetic multimedia content—which, combined with the pseudonymity of cryptoassets, constitute a growing vector for financial fraud and cybercrime. Schemes such as *pig butchering*, *rug pulls*, market manipulation, and identity fraud are amplified by AI, resulting in global financial losses reaching US$ 79.1 billion since 2022. This research highlights that Brazil is among the top ten countries with the highest number of deepfake-based attacks, especially within the cryptocurrency segment. In response, Brazil has adapted its legislation, notably Law No. 14,478/2022, which regulates Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs), enhances AML/CFT protocols, and introduces the specific crime of virtual asset fraud (Art. 171-A of the Penal Code). This article critically analyzes the evolution of these risks and proposes strategic measures to strengthen national digital resilience.

**Keywords:** Deepfakes, Crypto-fraud, Artificial Intelligence, Financial Crime, Law 14,478/2022, Digital Law.

### 🇧🇷 Resumo (Português)
O avanço da inteligência artificial (IA) tem impulsionado a proliferação de deepfakes que, em conjunto com a pseudonimidade dos criptoativos, configuram um vetor crescente para fraudes financeiras. Esquemas como *pig butchering* e manipulação de mercado resultaram em perdas globais de US$ 79,1 bilhões desde 2022. O Brasil situa-se entre os dez países com maior número de ataques baseados em deepfakes. O artigo analisa a resposta legislativa brasileira, com destaque para a Lei nº 14.478/2022 e o novo tipo penal de fraude com ativos virtuais (Art. 171-A do Código Penal), discutindo os impactos operacionais e os desafios estratégicos para mitigar riscos em um ambiente de rápida evolução tecnológica.

**Palavras-chave:** Deepfakes, Criptofraudes, Inteligência Artificial, Crimes Financeiros, Lei 14.478/2022, Direito Digital.
